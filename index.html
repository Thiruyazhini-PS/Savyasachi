❖ VANDANA ❖
❖ GHRANA · SENSES OF THE WOLF-BELLIED

Bhima pauses before the ornate carved pillar. Beneath the heavy veil of camphor and saffron, his superhuman senses catch the unmistakable acrid bite of boiled shellac, tree lac, and crude petroleum pitch.

Bhima

"Brother... the joinery oozes amber oil under the torchlight. This entire palace is not a home—it is an engineered funeral pyre waiting for a midnight spark!"

❖ CHIT-CHIT ❖
Queen Kunti

"Vidura's riddle resounds in my soul: 'He who knows the weapon of fire cannot burn the creature deep in the womb of the earth.' We have stepped into Duryodhana's trap."

Yudhishthira

"We must feign total blindness, mother. If Purochana suspects we know, he will lock the doors tonight. We smile, enter, and locate the subterranean path out."
